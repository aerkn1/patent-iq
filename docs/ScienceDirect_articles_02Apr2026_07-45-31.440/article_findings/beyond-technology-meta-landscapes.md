# Beyond Technology Meta-landscapes: The Top 25 IPCs by Landscape Dynamics

## Why It Matters For PatentIQ
- Highly relevant for market intelligence and trend analytics, less directly for core forecast labels.
- It reinforces the value of structural field-level analytics across the full corpus rather than only family-level scores.

## Key Findings Relevant To PatentIQ
- The paper studies technology landscapes using dynamics such as:
  - growth
  - fragmentation
  - concentration
  - top-assignee expectation
- It treats technology landscapes as evolving systems, not static filing buckets.
- The fragmentation versus concentration lens is especially useful for strategic interpretation.

## PatentIQ Implications
- PatentIQ market intelligence can become stronger if it adds explicit landscape-structure metrics by field and jurisdiction:
  - fragmentation index
  - concentration index
  - top-owner expectation
  - acceleration or cooling by segment
- These metrics can enrich:
  - market intelligence overview
  - segment watchlists
  - portfolio context
  - forecast context features

## Good Ideas To Reuse
- Add field-level landscape structure features to `jurisdiction_field_trend_forecast`.
- Use fragmentation and concentration jointly, not separately.
- Compare segment maturity and owner crowding, not only filing volume.

## Cautions
- IPC meta-landscape logic is useful for context, but it should not displace the existing family-first product core.
- PatentIQ should adapt it through WIPO-field and segment-level marts, not by exposing raw IPC-heavy views everywhere.
