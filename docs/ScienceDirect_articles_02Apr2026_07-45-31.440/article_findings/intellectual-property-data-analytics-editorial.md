# Intellectual Property Data Analytics for Innovation, R&D, and Strategic Management

## Why It Matters For PatentIQ
- This is an editorial framing paper, not a benchmark study, but it is useful for confirming the research agenda around AI in IP analytics.

## Key Findings Relevant To PatentIQ
- Highlights the breadth of AI use in IP:
  - retrieval
  - comparison
  - classification
  - policy and law implications
  - claim generation and drafting support
- Notes the known issues with LLM judgment quality:
  - bias
  - inconsistency
  - limited transparency
- Mentions structured comparison and SAO-style semantic analysis as promising directions.

## PatentIQ Implications
- PatentIQ should keep structured, auditable intermediate representations in any LLM-supported workflow.
- It supports expanding beyond search and scoring into:
  - structured comparison
  - drafting QA
  - semantic decomposition
  - analyst copilots

## Good Ideas To Reuse
- Keep human-readable structured representations between raw text and LLM reasoning.
- Prefer dimension-wise comparison outputs over free-form narrative-only outputs.

## Cautions
- Because this is an editorial overview, it is best used for scope validation, not as evidence for one specific implementation choice.
